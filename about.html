About Fresko Universal

Trade relationships need trust.
Trust needs a clear record.

We are building Fresko Universal around a simple idea: fresh produce trading should stay relationship-driven without becoming information-blind.

Beyond boundaries is an operating idea.

Cross-border produce trade connects farms and exporters, shipping lines, import documentation, cold stores, market buyers, institutions, transporters and finance. The value is not merely in connecting two parties; it is in helping the chain move with fewer blind spots.

The product is perishable. The record should not be.

What we are building around

  • Commercial clarity
    Know the product, LOT, buyer, quantity and commercial context.
  • Operational traceability
    Connect receipt, QC, sale, dispatch and reconciliation.
  • Market responsiveness
    Fresh produce changes quickly; decisions need current ground truth.
  • Long-term counterparties
    Build repeatable exporter and buyer relationships, not anonymous transactions.
Our direction

A trade company with an operating-system mindset.

We use structured internal workflows to reduce dependence on fragmented chats and spreadsheets as the business grows. The technology supports the trade; it is not the trade.
