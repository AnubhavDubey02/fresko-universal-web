Exporter FAQ

Questions exporters ask

Do you publish fixed buying programs?

Fresh-produce programs depend on season, product, quality, pricing and current Indian demand. Send the shipment or program details for a current discussion.

Can you work with container-level and LOT-level information?

Yes. Fresko Universal’s operating approach is designed around container and LOT context through QC, sales, dispatch and reconciliation.

What should I send in a first enquiry?

Product, origin, variety, grade, pack, volume, shipping window, commercial terms and supporting product/QC material are the best starting point.
