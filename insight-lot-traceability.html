Why LOT-level traceability matters in fresh produce

A container tells you how the shipment travelled. A LOT helps tell you what happened to the product inside the operation.

The practical problem

Perishable stock can differ by packing, condition, grade or operational treatment. When sales are recorded without a clear stock reference, teams can struggle to answer a basic question: exactly which inventory did this deal consume?

A useful chain

LOT → QC → sale → dispatch → remaining quantity → payment reconciliation. Keeping these events connected makes exception handling easier and creates a more reliable commercial record.

Why buyers and exporters should care

Traceability does not replace relationships or inspection. It gives those relationships better facts: what arrived, what was observed, what moved and what remains.
